Services

From first visit to planning decision, in four steps.

  1. Initial consultation

    After your enquiry I visit the site to assess feasibility. I explain the process and expected timeframes, and give you a clear fixed price for the planning and design stage. I do not charge VAT.

  2. Survey and design

    I measure and level your existing building, then produce precise scale drawings of your proposal. We review them together and I make any changes needed until you are happy with the design.

  3. Planning application

    I manage the whole application for you: completing the forms, submitting the drawings and acting as your agent. I stay in contact with the planning authority until a decision is reached.

  4. Approval and documentation

    Once permission is granted, you receive your approved drawings as PDFs and as printed copies.
