About

I am based in Bovey Tracey, Devon. I studied Architectural Technology at Plymouth University and graduated in 2016. In 2020 I became a Chartered Architectural Technologist, a protected designation and regulated professional qualification.

I have worked in the architecture industry ever since, across a range of sectors and on projects throughout the South West.

What is a Chartered Architectural Technologist?

A Chartered Architectural Technologist makes sure building projects are designed and built to a high technical standard, using both innovative and traditional methods. They make sure the built environment meets the needs of people and planet, from accessibility to sustainability.
